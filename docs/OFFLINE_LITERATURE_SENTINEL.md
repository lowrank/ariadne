# Offline Research with a Literature Sentinel

## Purpose

Known literature can be useful but can also anchor a system to human conventions, inherited errors, or a familiar route that is not optimal. Ariadne therefore separates first-principles route formation from literature comparison.

## Phase A: blind route formation

One or more offline researchers receive no literature dossier and no Ariadne-provided online search tools. Each declares:

- method family;
- representation;
- key bridge lemma;
- central mechanism;
- decisive test;
- difference from other active routes.

The route summary is sufficient for coordination without revealing private chain-of-thought.

## Phase B: sentinel comparison

The literature sentinel receives route summaries and exact source objects. It may issue an intervention when:

- the route duplicates a known method;
- the method has a known obstruction;
- an exact result already resolves the route or target;
- a cited or remembered source does not match the project assumptions;
- submitted novelty evidence confirms the route is materially different.

The sentinel should send the minimum information needed to prevent wasted work. It should not replace the offline agent’s state with a literature survey.

## Phase C: negotiation

The offline agent can accept the stop or reject it. A rejection is valid only if it states a material difference and a decisive test.

Ariadne grants one provisional novelty window. During that window the route must produce evidence tied to the intervention ID. If it fails, the route is stopped as a known route. If it succeeds, the sentinel reviews the evidence and can withdraw the stop.

## Campaign-level stopping

A route-level literature stop does not automatically solve the problem. Even an `EXACT_RESULT_KNOWN` intervention should trigger exact source and applicability audit before the campaign is closed.

## Why the offline agent cannot read the cited source during negotiation

The first response asks whether the agent’s internally developed mechanism is structurally different. Giving the source immediately can cause retrospective conformity. After the route-difference decision is recorded, a later source-audit role may compare exact details.
